Build Your Rental: the Heavy/Med + Lights Blueprint

Every game in this rental is still yours to mix and match freely. To help you land on a good spread without over-ordering, we suggest a simple Blueprint built from the same Duration badges already on each row below: one Heavy game, or two Medium games, plus a handful of Light fillers, sized to how many days you're renting.

These are starting points, not hard limits — but before you can save, your ticks need to add up to at least one of: 1 Heavy, 2 Medium, 3 Light, or 1 Medium + 2 Light.

Why not just pick as many as fit?

Even with a whole day free, most groups won't play a new game just once — you usually need at least two plays to really understand it, or to decide whether you liked it. And a rental day still has to fit meals, sleep, and actually talking to each other, not just back-to-back games. This Blueprint keeps your picks focused on games you'll get real table time with, instead of a pile that goes untouched.
